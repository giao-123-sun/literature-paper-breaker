# 同行评审报告（第二轮）

**论文标题**：数字孪生与非物质文化遗产保护：人工智能时代传统手工艺知识图谱的构建与传承路径研究（修改稿）

## 编辑综合意见

### 综述

三位评审人一致认为修改稿在多个关键维度上取得了实质性改进：（1）补充了苏绣齐针的概念验证案例，回应了实证基础薄弱的批评；（2）对TCKO本体进行了形式化定义，包括能力问题、类层次结构和CIDOC-CRM映射表；（3）引入了具身认知理论（波兰尼、德雷福斯、英格尔德），深化了对隐性知识的理论理解；（4）嵌入了参与性设计维度，回应了传承人主体性问题。评审人对论文的修改方向和修改质量总体满意，建议进行小修后接受发表。

### 关键改进

- 基于设计科学研究方法论的明确方法论框架
- TCKO本体的形式化规范与CIDOC-CRM映射
- 苏绣齐针概念验证提供了实证支撑
- 传承人访谈数据增强了论文的实践关联性
- Kritzinger分类明确了CIIS的技术定位

### 剩余问题

1. 概念验证的规模仍然有限（仅1种针法），建议在终稿中讨论扩展计划
2. 部分数据互操作性的描述可以更加具体
3. 参与性设计维度的实施细节可进一步丰富

### 优点

1. **数字孪生概念的批判性反思**：正面回应了我关于"可预测性"与"创造性"矛盾的批评，提出的三类非遗数字孪生分类既实事求是又富有建设性
2. **概念验证的实证贡献**：齐针知识图谱虽然规模小（23个节点、47条边），但完整展示了从田野采集到TCKO实例化的技术路径
3. **传承人反馈的整合**：传承人A和B的直接引用不仅增添了论文的可读性，更重要的是揭示了技术设计需要回应的真实需求和限制
4. **数据采集可行性分析**：补充了轻量级（3000元）和高精度（50万元）两种采集方案的成本对比，使感知层的讨论更具操作性

### 不足

1. **概念验证的扩展性**：当前仅验证了一种基本针法。齐针是苏绣中最基础的技法，其知识结构相对简单。更复杂的技法（如打籽绣、乱针绣）是否能被TCKO有效表征，需要在终稿中至少进行讨论
2. **双向耦合的技术细节**：虽然CIIS定位于数字模型/影子层次的决策是合理的，但未来向数字孪生层次演进的技术路线图可以更清晰

### 修改建议

1. 在讨论部分增加对TCKO扩展性的讨论——当面对更复杂的技法时，本体可能需要哪些调整
2. 提供一个简要的技术路线图，说明从当前的数字模型/影子向数字孪生层次演进的关键技术节点

### 决定：**小修后接受**

1. 在参与性治理框架部分，补充"共同设计工作坊"的具体实施方案草案
2. 在应用层讨论中增加对"传承社会化"维度（不仅是技能传递，还包括文化认同和审美判断的养成）的分析

### 决定：**小修后接受**

## 评审人3：Chiara Boscaro

**Institution**: Politecnico di Milano | **Research Focus**: Digital Twins for Cultural Heritage | **h-index**: ~10

**Overall Score**: 7.8/10 | **Decision**: minor_revision

### Scoring

| Criterion | Score | Comment |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Originality | 7/10 | Maintained. The three-tier classification of ICH digital twins (descriptive/explanatory/assistive) is a useful conceptual addition |
| Theoretical Framework | 8/10 | Significantly improved. The integration of Dreyfus's skill acquisition model with differentiated digital representation strategies is theoretically productive. The Kritzinger classification properly positions CIIS within the DT spectrum |
| Methodology | 7/10 | Substantial improvement. The explicit adoption of DSR methodology and the inclusion of a proof-of-concept validation, however small, demonstrate methodological awareness. The field research with two master artisans adds empirical grounding |
| Literature Review | 8/10 | Now engages with Kritzinger's classification, FAIR principles, and heritage data standards (LIDO, Dublin Core, EDM). This addresses my primary literature concerns |
| Argumentation & Evidence | 8/10 | The proof-of-concept, while limited to a single stitch type, demonstrates the feasibility of the TCKO ontology with real data. The honest acknowledgment of limitations (craftsperson B's feedback on material-dependent variations) adds credibility |
| Writing Quality | 8/10 | Clear technical writing with improved precision in DT terminology |
| Significance | 8/10 | The paper now offers both a theoretical framework and initial empirical validation, positioning it as a more substantive contribution to the field |

### Strengths

1. **TCKO formalization**: The OWL-style class definitions, CIDOC-CRM mappings, and competency questions represent a genuine improvement in ontological rigor. The mapping table clearly showing how each TCKO class extends specific CRM entities (e.g., CraftProcess → E7_Activity) is exactly what was needed

2. **Proof-of-concept**: The QiZhen stitch knowledge graph (23 nodes, 47 edges) with RDF-style instantiation examples demonstrates that the ontology can be operationalized with real data. This moves the paper from pure conceptualization toward implementation

3. **DT terminology precision**: The adoption of Kritzinger's classification and the honest positioning of CIIS at the digital model/shadow level addresses my concern about imprecise DT terminology. The three-tier classification of ICH digital twins is a useful conceptual framework

4. **Data interoperability**: The discussion of LIDO, Dublin Core, and EDM interoperability addresses my concern about data standards, though further detail would be welcome

### Weaknesses

1. **Proof-of-concept scale**: The validation covers only one basic stitch type. QiZhen is the simplest Su embroidery technique; more complex techniques with richer decision structures would provide a more convincing test of TCKO's expressiveness

2. **Bidirectional coupling still aspirational**: While the honest DT positioning is appreciated, the paper could provide a more concrete technical roadmap for eventual bidirectional coupling — what sensors, what simulation platforms, what feedback mechanisms

3. **NeOn methodology compliance**: The paper mentions NeOn but does not fully follow its prescribed activities (e.g., formal evaluation against competency questions using SPARQL queries is not demonstrated)

### Suggestions

1. Add SPARQL query examples demonstrating how the knowledge graph answers the stated competency questions
2. Include a brief technical roadmap for the transition from digital model/shadow to digital twin, identifying specific technical milestones
3. Discuss how TCKO would handle the material-dependent variations identified by craftsperson B — this is an important design challenge

### Decision: **Minor Revision**
